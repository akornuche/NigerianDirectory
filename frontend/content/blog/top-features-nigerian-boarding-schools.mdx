Boarding schools offer a unique educational experience that goes beyond academics. When choosing a boarding school in Nigeria, parents need to consider factors that will shape their child's daily life for years. Here are the top 10 features to evaluate.

## 1. Accommodation Quality

The quality of dormitories directly impacts student wellbeing:
- Room capacity (avoid overcrowded dorms)
- Cleanliness and maintenance standards
- Proper ventilation and lighting
- Adequate storage for personal belongings
- Quality bedding and furniture

## 2. Food and Nutrition

A balanced diet is essential for growing students:
- Quality and variety of meals
- Dietary accommodation options
- Clean dining facilities
- Regular meal schedules
- Access to clean drinking water

## 3. Security Measures

Safety should be a top priority:
- Perimeter fencing and controlled access
- Security personnel presence
- Visitor management protocols
- Emergency procedures
- Health and safety policies

## 4. Medical Facilities

Healthcare access is crucial in boarding environments:
- On-campus sick bay or clinic
- Qualified medical staff
- Proximity to hospitals
- Mental health support
- Emergency response capabilities

## 5. Academic Excellence

Strong academics remain fundamental:
- Teacher qualifications
- Examination pass rates
- Subject variety
- Academic support systems
- Library and learning resources

## 6. Extracurricular Activities

Well-rounded development requires:
- Sports facilities and programs
- Arts and cultural activities
- Clubs and societies
- Leadership opportunities
- Community service programs

## 7. Pastoral Care

Emotional support systems matter:
- House parent/matron quality
- Counseling services
- Peer support programs
- Communication with parents
- Handling of homesickness

## 8. Communication Infrastructure

Parents need to stay connected:
- Phone call schedules
- Parent visit policies
- Email or messaging systems
- Regular progress reports
- Emergency contact protocols

## 9. Religious and Moral Education

Values formation is important:
- Chapel/mosque facilities
- Religious instruction
- Character development programs
- Moral guidance
- Community values

## 10. Alumni Network and Reputation

Long-term value matters:
- Graduate success stories
- Alumni associations
- School reputation
- University placement rates
- Industry connections

## How to Evaluate These Features

When visiting boarding schools:
1. Request a dormitory tour
2. Ask to see the dining hall during meal times
3. Review security protocols
4. Meet the medical staff
5. Talk to current students if possible
6. Request academic performance data
7. Ask about typical weekend activities
8. Understand the disciplinary system
9. Review the communication policy
10. Connect with alumni parents

## Making the Right Choice

The right boarding school provides not just education but a formative life experience. Take your time, visit multiple schools, and involve your child in the decision-making process.
